This codex is a structural excavation of `github.com/toxicwind`, a 233-repository estate spanning NVIDIA-NIM swarm orchestration, Python AST supply-chain audit, GitHub-API automation, and OSINT dossiers. The corpus is not a coherent product. It is a working notebook of a single practitioner shipping infrastructure at the edge, and this documentation reads it as such: as sedimentary rock, not as a triumph.

The user's self-description ("Security research, AI infrastructure, autonomous systems, 12\+ years shipping code at the edge, digital studio @ effusionlabs.com") is honest. What the profile actually contains, once you strip away the 100\+ upstream forks used as scratch spaces, is a small nucleus of original tools written in the Gwern-adjacent tradition: single-file, dependency-minimal, AST-first, and openly contemptuous of the ecosystems they parse.
